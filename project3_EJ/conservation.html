Wildlife Conservation

Denver Zoo helps protect animals through conservation programs, habitat protection, and public education. Zoos play an important role in helping endangered species survive and teaching people about wildlife around the world.

Visitors can support conservation by reducing pollution, recycling, protecting habitats, and learning about endangered animals. Even small actions can make a difference for wildlife.

The zoo also works with animal care specialists, veterinarians, and conservation scientists to improve the lives of animals both in captivity and in the wild. Education programs encourage future generations to care about protecting nature.
